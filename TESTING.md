# Playtest 3: what to try first (Nov 21, 2026)

Build `playtest-3`, commit 182074dd6. Apple Silicon Macs only. About 10 minutes for each step.

1. **A first game.** New Game, House Ostral, Gentle. Follow the guide past the first Winter Council; it should end by itself after that council. Leave the steward on: your house should not go hungry before the first harvest.
2. **Season plan.** Options > Game > Time > Season plan (or on the New Game screen). Rank the seasons' needs, look at the forecast, then "Watch the season". Is 1.2 seconds a day the right speed? Does it stop at the right moments?
3. **Against the computer.** New Game > Against the computer: your family plus 3 houses, Quick, Normal. Watch the race strip, answer an offer under "Other houses", play to the end screen. Does it feel fair? Too easy or too hard?
4. **Hot-seat.** Same setup with "People at this screen" set to 2. End a turn, pass the screen, read the "While you waited" card and press "Show me".
5. **The families.** Try Duro (strong) or Fennick (winter). Open "Old Grudges" under More: read the grudges and use the family's deed once a season.
6. **Look and read.** Watch the village at normal zoom: no names or speech should print over each other or show through panels. Read the "How to play" page in the menu.

## Known rough edges
- In hot-seat only the last person to end a turn watches the days play out; the others get the card.
- "Show me" buttons are gone after loading a save.
- Lines that someone just spoke keep their place if you move the camera.
- Ranking trade low in the season plan does not yet stop the steward trading with the merchant.
- Online play is not built (on hold by choice).
