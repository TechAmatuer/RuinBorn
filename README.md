# RuinBorn — test builds

A medieval survival game: keep your family alive through the winters, dig old knowledge out of the ruins, and pass it on to your children.

## Download (Mac)

**Newest test build: playtest 2 (Nov 21, 2026).** [Download RuinBorn-playtest-2.zip](https://github.com/TechAmatuer/RuinBorn/raw/playtest/downloads/RuinBorn-playtest-2.zip) (77 MB, Apple Silicon and Intel Macs).

1. Double-click the zip to unpack it. It contains **RUINBORN.app**.
2. Older builds: open **Releases** on the right of this page.

### New in playtest 2
- You look down on your valley and click places to act; days pass only when the head of the house acts.
- Knowledge lives in people: a gold thread joins teacher and student, a flame marks the last who knows a craft, and a death shows what was lost.
- A strip above the action bar shows food, firewood, crafts at risk and the race; warnings come before trouble.
- A clearer Winter Council with costs shown before each choice; rival raids you answer yourself; rival letters.
- Six families to choose from, old choices that come back years later, villagers with their own lines.
- Match mode: 2-6 houses at one screen, 20 to 40 minutes.

## First time you open it

Your Mac blocks apps from developers it doesn't know. Double-click RUINBORN, click **Done**, then go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. After that it opens normally.

This is an early test build: simple art, and things may break. To report a problem: **More > Report a problem** in the game, then send Victor the zip it makes.
