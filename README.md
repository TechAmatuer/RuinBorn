# RuinBorn — test builds

A medieval survival game: keep your family alive through the winters, dig old knowledge out of the ruins, and pass it on to your children.

## Download (Mac)

**Newest test build: playtest 3 (Nov 21, 2026).** [Download RuinBorn-playtest-3.zip](https://github.com/TechAmatuer/RuinBorn/raw/playtest/downloads/RuinBorn-playtest-3.zip) (74 MB, **Apple Silicon Macs only**: M1 and newer).

1. Double-click the zip to unpack it. It contains **RUINBORN.app**.
2. What to try first: [TESTING.md](TESTING.md).
3. Older build: [playtest 2](https://github.com/TechAmatuer/RuinBorn/raw/playtest/downloads/RuinBorn-playtest-2.zip) (77 MB, Apple Silicon and Intel).

### New in playtest 3
- **Against the computer, in your valley:** 1 to 5 other houses, Easy, Normal or Hard, Friendly, Classic or Cutthroat. A race strip shows every house's Great Work and Renown; answer their offers of trade, alliance and marriage; win by finishing your Great Work first.
- **Several people at one screen (hot-seat):** each person plays their own valley, then passes the screen. A "While you waited" card shows what changed, with "Show me" to see where.
- **Season plan:** a new way to play. Rank what matters each season, pin people to jobs, see a forecast, then watch the season; it stops when something needs you.
- **The six families have their own story:** old grudges between every pair of houses, a storyline and ending for each family, one special deed a season (the Duros are the strong house, the Fennicks the winter house), 36 new council events.
- **The computer houses have character:** they remember grudges, refuse old enemies, raid on a Cutthroat table and write to you in their own voice.
- Painted pictures on the council and story cards, and on the title screen.
- The steward no longer lets a new house go hungry before its first harvest.
- Many fixes from play-testing: the first-year guide, overlapping names and speech, stale warnings, and names showing through panels.

## First time you open it

Your Mac blocks apps from developers it doesn't know. Double-click RUINBORN, click **Done**, then go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. After that it opens normally.

This is an early test build: simple art, and things may break. To report a problem: **More > Report a problem** in the game, then send Victor the zip it makes.
